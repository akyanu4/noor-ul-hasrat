ANIMATED SHORTS: NIZAM-UL-AMAL-E-FUQARA, PART 6 (NAF006.mp3)
Hazrat Azam Pasha R.A.'s own voice. 5 Shorts, 51 scenes, one scene for each sentence or short thought.
For each scene: (1) use the IMAGE named (it is already in this folder as <CODE>.png), or make a new one from the
prompt, then (2) animate it into a VIDEO CLIP with the MOTION prompt.

====================================================================
HOW TO DO IT
====================================================================
1. IMAGE: most scenes already have their starting picture here: N6S1-01.png, N6S1-02.png ... (9:16, cropped
   from the pictures of the earlier Shorts). Scenes marked  NEW IMAGE  have only a stand-in picture: paste the
   image prompt (+ STYLE) into ChatGPT or Gemini, ask for portrait 9:16, and save it over the stand-in with the
   same name (for example N6S1-03.png).
2. CLIP: open an image-to-video tool, for example:
     - Kling AI (klingai.com) -> Image to Video   (choose 10 seconds; 5 s is enough for scenes under 8 s)
     - Gemini app -> Video (Veo): upload the image, paste the motion prompt  (8-second clips)
     - Hailuo (hailuoai.video) or Runway also work
   Choose 9:16. Save it as  N6S1-01.mp4, N6S1-02.mp4 ...  in this same folder:
     tools/shorts/projects/2026-11-nizam-ul-amal-6/clips/
   Any sound or music the tool adds is ignored; Hazrat's voice is used.
   A clip shorter than its scene is slowed (down to 0.6x), then its last frame holds with a slow zoom. So a
   10 s clip covers up to about 16.5 s. Scenes marked (long) are a little longer than that; a 10 s clip is fine.
3. Until a clip is made, the Short uses the still picture with a slow zoom, so it always renders.
   Tell me when the clips are in; I render the Shorts with the voice, captions, title, end card and credits.

CHECK EVERY CLIP before keeping it: the shaykh's face must stay covered by light from the first frame to the
last, no extra hands or fingers, no letters or signs appearing anywhere, no women appearing in the crowds,
nobody suddenly changing clothes. If a clip goes wrong, generate it again (or delete it: the still is used).

RULES (keep in every prompt)
- Never draw the Prophet (peace be upon him). Never draw Allah's name or any calligraphy.
- NO WRITING AT ALL. The AI must not draw letters, Arabic, Urdu, book titles, shop signs or captions. The words
  of the dars (Ya Khallaqu, Ya Fa''alu, Ya Musawwiru; Allah; Tawheed; the verse) are NOT drawn by the AI.
- HAZRAT (the shaykh): face ALWAYS hidden by soft white glowing light. No facial features. The glow stays over
  his face in every frame of the clip.
- Shaytan and bad thoughts: ONLY as dark smoke or shadow. Never a figure, never a face in the smoke.
- People in streets and crowds: only men, seen from behind or the side, faces out of focus.
- No symbols of other religions (no temples, crosses, idols, Sikh or Hindu signs).

THE CHARACTERS (copy into a prompt whenever they appear)
====================================================================

no text, no letters, no writing, no signs, no logos, no women"
