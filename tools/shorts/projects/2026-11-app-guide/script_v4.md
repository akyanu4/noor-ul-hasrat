# App guide, Version 4 (new features), two languages

Voice for both: Google AI Studio → Generate speech, single speaker, voice **Daro**, style **Friendly** (same as before).
- English version: style prompt "Read in English, warm and calm, unhurried, with short pauses between sentences."
- Urdu version: style prompt "Read in Urdu, warm and calm, unhurried, with short pauses between sentences. English words are said the way Hyderabadi speakers say them."
  (The Urdu text is in Urdu script so the voice pronounces it well; the on-screen captions are in Roman Urdu.)

Generate each numbered part as its own clip and save it as shown (e.g. `EN_W01.wav`).

---
## English: walkthrough (about 4 minutes)

EN_W01. Assalamu alaikum. Welcome to Noor-ul-Hasrat, a way to stay connected with the teachings of Qadeer Piya Sarkar, by Hazrat Azam Pasha R.A. Let us see how to use it.

EN_W02. At the top of Home, choose your language: English or Roman Urdu. The whole app follows your choice.

EN_W03. Below it is the Saying of the day, a short saying of Hazrat. Tap Listen to hear it in his own voice, Read more for the full teaching, or Share to send it on WhatsApp. Every morning, it also comes as a notification.

EN_W04. New here? Start 7 Days with Noor-ul-Hasrat. Each day opens one short video and one reading. Just a few minutes a day.

EN_W05. Events shows the coming gatherings. Every Saturday, the Halqa-e-Zikr is led by Hazrat Noor Uddin Siddiqui, Nayeem Baba. Join in person or online, and tap Remind me.

EN_W06. In Dars Highlights, Your path shows Hazrat's dars in the right order. Videos play full screen. Swipe for the next one, and share any of them with a friend.

EN_W07. The Library explains Hazrat's teachings in simple articles, in English and Roman Urdu, approved by our teachers. Each saying has a Watch button that plays that exact moment of the dars.

EN_W08. Learn Sufi Practices takes you lesson by lesson. Watch, read, answer a few questions, and write what you understood. Send it to your teacher, who replies and opens the next lesson. Remember, these practices are effective only under the guidance of a Shaykh.

EN_W09. To take Bai'at, open Taking Bai'at for the steps in Hazrat's own words, then send a request from the app. A Khalifa will contact you, in shaa Allah.

EN_W10. Prayer times, Qibla, the Islamic calendar, the Qur'an with bookmarks and recitation, Duas, and Tasbeeh with the whole community's count. All in one place.

EN_W11. Tap Sign in at the top to create your free account, with just your email and no password. Members keep their Iman Health safe, get reminders, and see this week's rewards and offers from our vendors.

EN_W12. To invite someone, tap Share Hazrat's teachings at the bottom of Home, and show them the QR code. And to keep the app one tap away, tap Get app and add it to your phone.

EN_W13. Noor-ul-Hasrat is free, for everyone. May Allah keep our hearts alive with His remembrance. Ameen.
