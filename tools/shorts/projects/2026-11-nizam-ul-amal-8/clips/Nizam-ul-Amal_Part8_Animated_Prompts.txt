HOW TO DO IT
====================================================================
1. IMAGE: 69 scenes already have their starting picture in this folder (N8S1-01.png ...), 9:16, 1080x1920,

   21 scenes are marked  IMAGE: NEW  and have NO picture yet (the Short shows a dark "missing" card there).
   For each NEW one: open a NEW Gemini chat (one picture per chat), paste the prompt from "NEW PICTURES TO MAKE"
   at the end of this file, ask for portrait 9:16, a single image; save it here with the exact name
   (for example N8S1-04.png).
2. CLIP: open an image-to-video tool, for example:
     - Gemini app -> Video (Veo): upload the image, paste the motion prompt  (8-second clips)
     - Kling AI (klingai.com) -> Image to Video                           (5 or 10 seconds)
     - Hailuo (hailuoai.video) or Runway also work
   Choose 9:16 if asked. Save it as  <CODE>.mp4  in this same folder
   (tools/shorts/projects/2026-11-nizam-ul-amal-8/clips/).
   Any sound or music the tool adds is ignored; Hazrat's voice is used.
   Until a clip exists, the Short uses the png with a slow zoom, so it always renders.
3. Tell me when pictures or clips are in; I re-render (voice, captions, title, end card, credits, watermark).

Length: a 10 s clip covers up to about 16 s of voice (it is slowed down to 0.6x, then the last frame holds).
Scenes over 10 s: choose 10 s in Kling. Scenes under 8 s: 5 s in Kling or 8 s in Veo is fine.

CHECK EVERY PICTURE AND CLIP before keeping it: faces of the shaykh, the pir, the awliya, the elders and Hazrat Musa
Pasha must stay fully covered by opaque light the whole time (face, beard and ears); dark smoke must stay plain smoke
(no faces, horns, eyes or figures in it); no women anywhere; no extra hands or fingers; NO WRITING of any kind (no
letters, no Arabic, no Qur'an pages with script, no signs, no numbers); no oil lamps, diyas or flames on floors or
ledges; nobody changing clothes. If it goes wrong, make it again (or tell me and I keep the still image).

RULES (keep in every prompt)
- Never draw the Prophet (peace be upon him): only light, the Green Dome, the Jali with no one behind it. No dream
  scene of him. No other prophet is drawn either (Hazrat Zakariya, Hazrat Yahya): only places, objects and light.
- Never draw Allah or anything that reads as Allah in a form: no single central orb or disc under the word Allah or
  under His Names; the search-light comes from clouds, with no visible source.
- No women at all. Bibi Maryam is never shown, not even veiled or from behind: only the room, the curtain, the
  fruit and the light.
- The shaykh, the pir, any wali or elder, Hazrat Musa Pasha: face, beard and ears ALWAYS hidden by soft opaque
  white light. People on the ship: only small, from far away or from behind.
- Shaytan, the mu'akkils, khatrat: only thin dark grey smoke or shadow. Never a figure, never a face.
- No diyas or oil-lamp flames on floors or ledges, no kalava threads, no figurines, no symbols of other traditions.
- No writing made by the AI. The real words (the four Names هُوَ مِنْ عِنْدِ اللّٰهِ, صوم مریم, حزب البحر ...) are drawn
  by the engine over the clip from clip_words.py, never by the AI.
- No Mushaf (Qur'an) on the ground.
