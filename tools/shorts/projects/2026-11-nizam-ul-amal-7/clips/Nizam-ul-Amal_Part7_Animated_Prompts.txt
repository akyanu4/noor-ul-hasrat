ANIMATED SHORTS: NIZAM-UL-AMAL-E-FUQARA, PART 7 (NAF007.mp3)
Hazrat Azam Pasha R.A.'s own voice. Five Shorts, 64 scenes. One scene per sentence or short thought.
For each scene: (1) the IMAGE (most are already in this folder), then (2) animate it into a VIDEO CLIP with the MOTION prompt.

====================================================================
HOW TO DO IT
====================================================================
1. IMAGE: every scene already has a starting picture in this folder:  N7S1-01.png ... N7S5-12.png  (9:16, 1080x1920).
   - Scenes marked  IMAGE: NEW  have only a stand-in picture (small or not quite right). Paste the image prompt
     into ChatGPT or Gemini, ask for portrait 9:16, and save it OVER the stand-in with the same name (N7S1-01.png ...).
   - All other scenes: use the png as it is.
2. CLIP: open an image-to-video tool, for example:
     - Gemini app -> Video (Veo): upload the image, paste the motion prompt  (8-second clips)
     - Kling AI (klingai.com) -> Image to Video                           (5 or 10 seconds)
     - Hailuo (hailuoai.video) or Runway also work
   Choose 9:16 if asked. Save it as  N7S1-01.mp4, N7S1-02.mp4 ...  in this same folder
   (tools/shorts/projects/2026-11-nizam-ul-amal-7/clips/).
   Any sound or music the tool adds is ignored; Hazrat's voice is used.
   Until a clip exists, the Short uses the png with a slow zoom, so it always renders.
3. Tell me when clips are in; I re-render (voice, captions, title, end card, credits, watermark).

Length: a 10 s clip covers up to about 16 s of voice (it is slowed down to 0.6x, then the last frame holds).
Scenes over 10 s: choose 10 s in Kling. Scenes under 8 s: 5 s in Kling or 8 s in Veo is fine.

CHECK EVERY CLIP before keeping it: the shaykh's and the wali's faces must stay covered by light the whole time,
the dark smoke must stay plain smoke (no faces, horns, eyes or figures in it), no extra hands or fingers,
NO WRITING of any kind (no letters, no Arabic, no Qur'an pages with script, no signs), nobody changing clothes.
If a clip goes wrong, generate it again (or tell me and I keep the still image with a slow zoom).

RULES (keep in every prompt)
- Never draw the Prophet (peace be upon him). No companions shown: for the companions' pact, hands only.
- The shaykh and any wali: face ALWAYS hidden by soft white glowing light, no facial features.
- Shaytan / bad thoughts (khatrat): only as thin dark grey smoke or shadow. Never a figure, never a face.
- No writing made by the AI: no calligraphy, no Arabic or Urdu letters, no book pages with script, no shop signs.
  The real words (Ud'uni astajib lakum, ad-du'a mukhkh al-'ibadah, Allah, panah, the verse
  "wa ya'ti bi-khalqin jadid") are NOT drawn by the AI; the captions carry them (see the note at the end).
- No symbols of other traditions. Short 5 stays light and abstract: no people except scene 12 (seen from behind).
- Other people: seen from behind or the side, or ordinary faces; never the shaykh.
