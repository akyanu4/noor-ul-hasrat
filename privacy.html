Privacy

Noor-ul-Hasrat is free. There is no sign-up, no ads, and nothing is sold. Last updated 12 October 2026.

In short: your worship record, bookmarks, reading progress and voice recordings stay on your phone. Only what is listed under "What leaves your phone" is sent anywhere.

Stored only on your phone

  • Iman Health: prayers you mark, fasting, dhikr counts, good deeds, zakat (paid or not, never an amount) and your saved masjid places.
  • Qur'an and reading: bookmarks, last page and reading progress. Tasbeeh counts and settings.
  • Voice counting: the microphone is used only while you switch it on. Sound is analysed on the phone and is never recorded to a file or uploaded. The few short phrase patterns you teach it are kept on the phone.

Clearing the site data in your browser, or using "Erase" in Iman Health, removes these.

Location

If you allow it, the app uses your location while it is open to calculate prayer times and to check whether you are at a masjid you saved. This check happens on your phone. Your coordinates are not sent to us. You can turn off "Mark it for me" in Iman Health or the location permission in your phone settings at any time.

What leaves your phone

  • Prayer-time notifications (only if you turn them on): OneSignal receives a device token, the name of the nearest big city (for example "Hyderabad") and a switch saying you want nudges. It does not receive your exact location or your worship record.
  • Live features: shared dhikr counts and RSVPs for the weekly halqa use Firebase. If sign-in is enabled it is anonymous: no name, email or phone number.
  • Videos and fonts: YouTube (embedded videos and thumbnails) and Google Fonts load from Google, which may see your IP address as any website would. Videos use the privacy-enhanced YouTube player.
  • Qur'an recitation: audio streams from mp3quran.net when you press play.
  • Hosting: the app is hosted on Vercel, which keeps ordinary server logs.

What we do not do

  • No accounts, no tracking across other sites, no advertising, and no selling or sharing of your data.

Your choices

  • Turn off notifications in Iman Health or your phone settings.
  • Deny location or microphone; the rest of the app still works.
  • Clear the app's data in your browser to remove everything stored on the phone.
